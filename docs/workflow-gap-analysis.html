4. Deep Dive: The 7 Critical Gaps Blocking Our Intended Workflow

Gap 1 — Video Metadata Blindness & Highlight Deadlock P0 BLOCKER

Evidence: sidecar/engine/Sources/engine/main.swift:245-261, 568-745, 1147-1155

  • Why 0/34 Moab videos have dates or GPS: videoStat() only checks asset.creationDate?.stringValue with a strict ISO-8601 parser and never reads asset.creationDate?.dateValue, QuickTime keys (com.apple.quicktime.creationdate, com.apple.quicktime.location.ISO6709), or Android/MP4 filename timestamps (video_YYYYMMDD_HHMMSS.mp4).
  • Why make_highlight hangs on real MP4s:
    1. background() blocks the main thread in done.wait() while AVFoundation’s Fig inspector dispatches callbacks to the main queue.
    2. aOut = AVAssetReaderTrackOutput(track: aTrack, outputSettings: nil) reads compressed AAC packets, then feeds them into AVAssetWriterInput configured with kAudioFormatMPEG4AAC output settings (which requires uncompressed Linear PCM input).

Gap 2 — Trip vs. Micro-Event Fragmentation P0 BLOCKER

Evidence: src-tauri/src/db.rs:1388-1394, src-tauri/src/lib.rs:968,1600, src/routes/+page.svelte:2914

  • Our approach: Consecutive assets stay in one event only if (taken_at - last_time) <= gap_seconds && haversine_m <= 500.0.
  • Why it fails on trips: On a road trip or hike, moving 600 meters splits the event even if taken 60 seconds later and even if the user sets Max gap = 48h in the UI! In fotoflow.db, Apr 18 in Moab is fractured into 15 separate events (10 of them single-photo events along US-191).
  • Competitor approach: Two-tier hierarchy: Trip (multi-day, 30–50 km radius or away-from-home span: "Moab Trip · Apr 17–20") containing Moments / Stops (500m–2km stops within each day).

Gap 3 — Auto-Best Selection Lacks Day / Place / Person Coverage P0 WORKFLOW GAP

Evidence: src-tauri/src/db.rs:1656-1667, src/routes/+page.svelte:589-623, 1951

  • Current event_top_picks: Pure SQL ORDER BY COALESCE(a.quality_score, -1) DESC LIMIT 12.
  • What goes wrong:
    • Laplacian variance rewards high-frequency textures (rocks, foliage) over portraits with shallow depth-of-field (bokeh).
    • Can pick 10 burst/near-duplicate shots from the same 2-minute stop while leaving 4 other stops and entire days with zero photos.
    • Ignores favorite = 1, ignores named faces (face_clusters), and never picks videos (quality_score IS NULL → -1).

Gap 4 — Memory Video Is a Blind 60s Slice, Not a Story P1 WORKFLOW GAP

Evidence: src-tauri/src/lib.rs:1915-1961, src-tauri/src/db.rs:1621-1626, main.swift:557-790

  • Current behavior: export_highlight passes all event assets ordered by a.path to makeHighlight, which takes 3s per item until cursor.seconds >= 60 (~20 items) and drops the remaining 140 items!
  • What goes wrong:
    • Includes blurred and duplicate photos in the montage.
    • Only covers the first ~20 filenames of an event — never reaches afternoon/evening or later days.
    • No Ken Burns subtle zoom/pan on stills, no crossfade transitions, and no Day/Place title card overlays.

Gap 5 — Collective Multi-Contributor Pooling P1 WORKFLOW GAP

Evidence: src-tauri/src/db.rs:343-393, src-tauri/src/lib.rs:2545-2690

  • When friends & family pool photos from 3–5 phones/cameras into a shared trip:
    • No contributor / source-folder tag: You can’t see or filter whose phone a photo came from or ensure every contributor’s shots are represented.
    • Clock-skew & filename fallback: WhatsApp/shared files or cameras with wrong timezones lack EXIF or shift by hours; we don’t parse IMG_YYYYMMDD_HHMMSS / video_YYYYMMDD_HHMMSS when EXIF is missing.
    • Group representation: No "Make sure everyone in our travel group appears at least N times" quota in curation.

Gap 6 — Classification & Post-Import Pipeline Gaps P1 USABILITY GAP

Evidence: src-tauri/src/lib.rs:924-1032

  • run_post_import runs cluster_events at line 968 before run_cluster_faces at line 1009, so initial event face counts are stale until re-clustered.
  • run_near_duplicates (dHash) and detect_pets are not run in run_post_import — the user has to know to click "Find similar photos" in Cleanup manually.
  • Videos get zero scene tags and zero visual embeddings (embed_batch and classify skip videos instead of sampling a keyframe).

Order album assets chronologically by capture timestamp (with optional manual reorder) so slideshows and highlight videos follow the trip’s real timeline.
